2. Data We Process

2.1 License Activation and Validation

Activating and validating your license sends the following to our license service, hosted on Google Cloud Functions in the United States (region us-central1):

  • Your license key, as issued by Lemon Squeezy on purchase.
  • A machine identifier, sent only when you activate. It is a SHA-256 hash of two values read from your Windows registry: the Windows installation identifier (MachineGuid) and your processor model name. It is a one-way hash: neither value can be recovered from it.
  • An instance identifier, returned by Lemon Squeezy at activation and sent back on each later validation and on deactivation.

We do not collect your name, your computer name, your Windows user name, your MAC address, your disk serial numbers, your installed memory or your core count. Four of those were used by an earlier version and were deliberately removed: they changed on their own and locked out paying customers.

Validation happens when the plugin starts and periodically while it runs, so that a license can be suspended if it is shared or refunded.

2.2 Abuse Prevention

To stop automated license-key guessing, our license service counts requests per IP address, per license key, per machine and per activation instance. Each of those identifiers is hashed with SHA-256 before it is stored; the stored record holds only that hash, a counter and a timestamp. Records are kept in Google Cloud Datastore.

2.3 Server Logs

Requests to our license service are logged with the source IP address, the User-Agent string, the HTTP method, the request path and a timestamp. License keys are never written to logs. Logs are held by Google Cloud Logging.

2.4 Map, Search and Routing Requests

Rendering a map sends map coordinates, a zoom level and a style identifier to MapTiler AG (Switzerland), which returns map tiles, fonts and sprites. Searching for a place sends the text you typed to the same provider. Tracing a route sends the coordinates of the stops you placed to openrouteservice, operated by HeiGIT gGmbH (Germany).

These requests carry your IP address to those providers, as any network request does. They are handled under those providers' own privacy policies. The rendering engine we use, MapLibre Native, contains no telemetry: it reports nothing about your usage to anyone, including us.

2.5 Payment and Billing Data

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). Lemon Squeezy processes billing and payment data under its own privacy policy and acts as an independent data controller for payment processing. We receive limited transaction metadata (such as order status and license key state) necessary for licensing. We never see your card details.

2.6 Support Communications

If you contact us, we process the information you provide (such as email content and attachments) to answer requests and provide support.

2.7 What Stays on Your Computer

The following are written to your machine and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your license session, in %APPDATA%\GoAtlasResolvePlugin\native-session.dat, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API and readable only by your Windows account on that machine.
  • A map cache, in %APPDATA%\GoAtlasResolvePlugin\map-cache.sqlite, holding map tiles already downloaded so that the same view is not paid for twice.
  • Your projects and the maps you create. They live in your DaVinci Resolve project and on your disk. We have no server that stores them, no account that syncs them, and no way to see them.
  • Diagnostic logs, in the same folder, recording what the Software did so that a fault can be investigated. The licence log deliberately records neither your license key nor your machine identifier — only a verdict, an HTTP status and a timestamp — precisely because a log is a plaintext file people attach to bug reports. Nothing in this folder is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it to us yourself.

2.8 The Website

getgoatlas.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics or behavioural tracking. Web fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts, so browsing these pages sends your IP address to no third party for the sake of a typeface.

One request does leave your browser: the pricing page asks our own counter function, hosted on Google Cloud, how many early-access seats remain. It conveys your IP address to Google as the host of that function, carries no identifier of any kind, and returns a number only.

  • License records: held by Lemon Squeezy for as long as the license exists, and thereafter as their retention policy and our accounting obligations require.
  • Accounting records: 10 years, as French commercial law requires.
  • Support emails: kept while the request is open and for a reasonable period afterwards.

7. Security

  • The map credentials the plugin needs are never written to disk in the clear and are never placed in the environment of a child process.
  • Identifiers used for rate limiting are hashed before storage.
  • Our API keys are held in Google Secret Manager, not in the software you download.

desirable to choose a license that granted us the same protections for new code
that were granted to the IJG for code derived from their software.
