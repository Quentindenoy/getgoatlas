More questions?

Do I need design or video skills?

If you've used any modern video editor — timeline, keyframes, export — you already know enough. Go Atlas© uses the same model, applied to maps.

Can I use the exported videos commercially?

Yes. You own your videos, commercially and without royalties. The map background is built from OpenStreetMap data, so the small © OpenStreetMap contributors credit stays visible in the corner of every export — required by their licence. Everything else is yours.

Do you collect my project data?

No. Your projects stay on your machine. We don't upload, store, or analyse the content of your scenes — there is no server that could hold them. What we process is your licence key, a one-way hash identifying your machine, and support emails when you reach out. Map views you render are fetched from Go Atlas’s own map service, and routes from openrouteservice. Every field, and what we deliberately do not collect, is listed in our Privacy Policy.

What machine do I need?

Go Atlas© is a real-time GPU-accelerated plugin that runs inside DaVinci Resolve, so it needs a working Resolve installation and a creator-grade machine, not a budget office laptop.

Minimum Recommended
Host DaVinci Resolve (sold separately by Blackmagic Design)
OS Windows 10 64-bit Windows 11 64-bit
CPU Intel i7-10700 / Ryzen 7 5700X Intel i7-13700+ / Ryzen 7 7700X
RAM 16 GB 32 GB
GPU NVIDIA RTX 3050 / AMD RX 6600 NVIDIA RTX 4060+ / AMD RX 7600 XT
Storage SSD, 5 GB free NVMe SSD, 10 GB free
Network Stable internet connection (map tiles)

Not supported Windows 7/8, macOS, Linux, HDDs, integrated graphics, GTX 16xx or older.

How often is Go Atlas© updated?

Frequently. Every release ships to every licence holder as soon as it's out, and an early-access licence includes updates for as long as Go Atlas© is published — there is nothing further to pay.
